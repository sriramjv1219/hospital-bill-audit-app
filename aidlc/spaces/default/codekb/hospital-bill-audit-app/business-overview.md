# Business Overview — hospital-bill-audit-app

## Summary

The repository `hospital-bill-audit-app` holds a single Next.js web application. **In code it is today an unmodified Create Next App scaffold.** It has one static page (`GET /`) showing the Next.js starter content. It implements no business functionality: no bill upload, no bill reading, no checks, no report and no letters.

The repository is the intended home of the Phase 1 "Hospital Bill Check" product described below. That product exists only in documents. Everything under "Intended product" is business context for later stages, **not a description of built software**.

## Intended product (context only — NOT YET BUILT)

> This section restates approved ideation decisions so that later stages read the codebase in context. None of it is implemented.

- **Product.** "Hospital Bill Check" (Project RS, Phase 1): a free, anonymous, single-session web check of Indian hospital bills. It reads the bill line by line, shows what may be worth asking the hospital about with evidence, and ends with a polite one-page Billing Desk Summary and ready-to-use letters.
- **Primary customers.** Self-paying patients and their families; the family member handling the bill at discharge; a remote family manager working from forwarded photos; patients who pay upfront and claim reimbursement later; bereaved families settling a bill. The hospital billing desk is a key stakeholder, not a user.
- **Session shape.** About 11 screens in one session: start, privacy and consent, the moment of checking, upload with a checklist, a quick summary, confirming unclear lines, the bill check with breakdown and findings, finding detail, the Billing Desk Summary, next steps and letters, and "before you go".
- **Fixed boundaries.** DPDP Act standards from day one; no accounts and a single session; original files deleted after reading; de-identified data kept for 3 months and only with consent; English only; no legal advice, no clinical opinion and no accusations; nothing may encourage withholding payment in a way that blocks discharge or treatment (C10).
- **Out of this build.** Pay-under-protest and urgent-situation guidance (moved to Phase 1.5), insurance checks, accounts, Hindi and regional languages, a native app.
- **Still undecided.** Hosting (Vercel is the working assumption only: "Mostly Vercel. But I would like to decide at a later point."), data residency, the bill-reading option (subject to a trial against accuracy targets of at least 95% on digital PDFs and at least 90% on good photos).

## Implications for later stages

- The application layer is effectively **greenfield** despite the Brownfield label: Requirements Analysis and design start from an empty domain, on a fixed, recent stack (see `technology-stack.md`).
- Template placeholders (metadata, home page copy, tracking links, README) must all be replaced before release (`code-quality-assessment.md`).
