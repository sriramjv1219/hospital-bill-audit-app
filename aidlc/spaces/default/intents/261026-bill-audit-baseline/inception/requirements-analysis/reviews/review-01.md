### Summary

Coverage of the PRD's Phase 1 Must Have and Should Have items is strong. Every item is either carried with PRD IDs or excluded with a source (S1, S9, S7, G-02 and the Could Have items), and no requirement contradicts a confirmed answer. Four Major issues block a clean sign-off: the discharge-urgent priority has no source of input, the stored-data field list conflicts with the aggregation requirement, the device and network target has no measurable test profile, and several requirements lack a pass/fail test. All are small edits.
