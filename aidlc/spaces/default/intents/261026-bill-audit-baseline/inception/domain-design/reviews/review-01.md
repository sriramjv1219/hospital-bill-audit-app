### Summary

The catalogue is structurally sound: it is acyclic, symmetric and complete, and the ADRs carry their alternatives and security consequences. The two Major gaps are the undeclared mechanism by which pinned rule data reaches CheckEngine, BillModel and FindingsReport (R-01), and the undefined failure rule for the close-time hand-over to ConsentedInsights (R-02). Both can be settled with a short clarification before Contract Design, so the verdict is READY, with R-01 and R-02 worth deciding at the gate.
