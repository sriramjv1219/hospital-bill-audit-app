### Summary

The refined mockups are thorough and faithful to Q1 to Q12: every Group 1 to 7 story maps to a screen, the fixed labels, "no anonymous" rule, bereavement wording and no-withholding constraint hold, and the contrast figures I recomputed are correct. Four items would send engineering back with questions: the missing "Fix a line" screen (R-01), the missing save reminder on discard (R-02), the inaccurate "no one else sees it" privacy line (R-03), and the unbounded pause of the idle timer during the user's own wait (R-04). Fix those, and the minor label mismatches can be settled in the same pass.
